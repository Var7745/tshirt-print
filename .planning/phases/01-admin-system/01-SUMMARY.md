# Phase 1 Summary: JK Empress Admin & Batch Production Engine

## Accomplishments
1. **Initial Order Status for Honeycomb Batch Creation**:
   - New batch orders start with status `photos_uploading` ("Photos Uploading") while students submit their images.
2. **Personalized Center "YOU" Rosette & Back Details**:
   - For every physical t-shirt, that member appears in the center ("YOU") surrounded by classmates on the front, and their jersey name, roll number, college, and department on the back.
3. **High-Resolution Single Garment Print Downloads**:
   - 300 DPI PNG and lossless Vector SVG downloads for Front and Back prints in Order Details and Central Print Files.
4. **Complete Multi-Garment Batch ZIP Archive**:
   - Single-click packaging of all 20 personalized garments into a `.zip` archive with production manifest.
5. **Admin Module Simplification**:
   - Decommissioned blank inventory, shipping, 4-side approvals, coupons, and staff routes with permanent safe redirects to `/admin/orders`.
